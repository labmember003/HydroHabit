# Droply Privacy Policy

_Effective: November 21, 2026_

Droply ("the app") is a water-tracking app published by Falcon Digital for Android and iOS. This policy explains what the app does with your information.

## The short version

Everything you enter in Droply stays on your device; we never receive it. The Android app shows a banner ad from Google AdMob, and Google's ad SDK collects some device data to show ads (see "Ads" below). The iPhone app has no ads.

## Information you enter

To calculate a daily water goal and track your progress, the app stores the following **on your device only**:

- Height, weight, gender and activity level
- Every drink you log (amount and time), your daily totals, streaks and unlocked achievements
- Reminder settings (interval, wake-up time, bedtime, notification sound)
- Display preferences such as units

This information is never sent to us or to any third party. The app has no accounts, no sign-in and no server.

## Apple Health (iOS, optional)

If you turn on **Settings → Save drinks to Health**, each drink you log is also saved to the Apple Health app on your iPhone as Water, and deleting a drink in Droply removes it from Health. Droply only writes Water to Health; it does not read any Health data. Health data is managed by Apple on your device and is never sent to us. You can turn this off at any time in Droply's Settings or in the Health app.

## Siri and Shortcuts (iOS)

The "Log Water" and "Today's Water" actions run on your device and use the same local data as the app. Nothing is sent to us.

## Notifications

Hydration reminders are scheduled locally on your device. No push-notification service or server is involved.

## Ads (Android app)

The Android app shows a banner ad above the tab bar, served by Google AdMob. To show and measure ads and to prevent fraud, Google's Mobile Ads SDK collects your device's IP address (used to estimate your general location), your Android advertising ID and app set ID, how you interact with ads (such as taps), and diagnostic information about the app's performance. This data goes to Google, not to us, and is encrypted in transit. It never includes your water log or body details. See how Google uses information from apps that use its services: https://policies.google.com/technologies/ads

In the EEA, the UK and Switzerland the app asks for your consent before ads are personalised, using Google's consent form; you can change your choice at any time in Settings → Ad privacy choices. You can reset or delete your advertising ID in your phone's Settings → Privacy → Ads.

The iPhone app has no ads.

## Analytics and tracking

Apart from the ad SDK in the Android app described above, the app contains no analytics or tracking SDKs, and we do not track you across apps or websites.

## Contacting us

If you use "Report a Bug" in Settings, your device's email app opens a new message to us. We only receive what you choose to send, and we use it solely to respond to you.

## Deleting your data

Uninstalling the app permanently deletes all data it stored on your device.

## Children

Droply is suitable for a general audience and does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children.
