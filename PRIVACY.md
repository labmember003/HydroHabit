# Droply Privacy Policy

_Effective: November 8, 2026_

Droply ("the app") is a water-tracking app published by Falcon Digital for Android and iOS. This policy explains what the app does with your information.

## The short version

Droply does not collect, transmit, sell or share any personal data. Everything you enter stays on your device.

## Information you enter

To calculate a daily water goal and track your progress, the app stores the following **on your device only**:

- Height, weight, gender and activity level
- Water intake logs, streaks and unlocked achievements
- Reminder settings (interval, wake-up time, bedtime, notification sound)
- Display preferences such as units

This information is never sent to us or to any third party. The app has no accounts, no sign-in and no server.

## Notifications

Hydration reminders are scheduled locally on your device. No push-notification service or server is involved.

## Analytics, advertising and tracking

The app contains no analytics, advertising or tracking SDKs, and it does not track you across apps or websites.

## Contacting us

If you use "Report a Bug" in Settings, your device's email app opens a new message to us. We only receive what you choose to send, and we use it solely to respond to you.

## Deleting your data

Uninstalling the app permanently deletes all data it stored on your device.

## Children

Droply is suitable for a general audience and does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children.

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted at this address with a new effective date.
