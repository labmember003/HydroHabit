- **GitHub:** [open an issue](https://github.com/labmember003/HydroHabit/issues)

## Common questions

**Reminders aren't arriving.**
Check that notifications are allowed for Droply in your phone's settings, and that the current time is between the wake-up time and bedtime you set in Droply's Settings.

**How is my daily goal calculated?**
From the height, weight, gender and activity level you enter during setup. You can change height and weight later in Settings.

**Where is my data stored?**
Only on your device. See the [privacy policy](PRIVACY.md).
