## Common questions

**Reminders aren't arriving.**
Check that notifications are allowed for Droply in your phone's settings, and that the current time is between the wake-up time and bedtime you set in Droply's Settings.

**How is my daily goal calculated?**
From the height, weight, gender and activity level you enter during setup. You can change height and weight later in Settings.

**I logged the wrong amount.**
Tap Undo right after adding it, or open History, find the drink under the day it was logged and tap the trash icon. Undo is offered there too.

**How do I sync with Apple Health?**
In Settings, turn on "Save drinks to Health" and allow Water in the permission sheet. If you chose "Don't Allow" earlier, open the Health app, tap your profile picture, then Apps, Droply, and turn on Water.

**Can I log water with Siri?**
Yes. Say "Log water in Droply" or "How much water have I had in Droply". You can also add these actions in the Shortcuts app.

**Where is my data stored?**
Only on your device. See the [privacy policy](PRIVACY.md).
