Takeaways:
- Every leader uses the exact pattern `Brand + water tracker/reminder keywords` — Droply's title follows the proven pattern.
- Head terms are winnable only long-term; early ranking will come from long-tail ("drink water reminder", "daily water tracker", "water drinking habit") where difficulty is ≤1.4.
- Differentiators to emphasize in creatives: **completely free** (leaders are freemium), body-personalized goal, sleep-aware reminders, custom sounds.

## Brand-Name Collision Check (why not HydroHabit)

- "HydroHabit" already has 4 App Store apps (two "HydroHabit: Water Tracker" + "HydroHabit" + "Hydro Habit Pro") → brand search unwinnable, Apple 4.1 confusion risk.
- Eliminated (iTunes Search, 2026-07-18): Sip (3×), Glug (2×), Waterly (2×), Droplet, WaterWise, Waterful (®), Aquify, AquaFlow (2×).
- **"Droply"** — clean in Health & Fitness on the App Store (user's pick, 2026-07-18); distinctive, brand-search winnable, 6 chars leaves 24 for keywords ("Droply: Water Tracker Reminder" = exactly 30).
